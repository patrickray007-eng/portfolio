# Patrick Ray

## Who He Is

Patrick Ray is a Houston-based program builder, startup advisor, and technical generalist with 20 years of work experience spanning mechanical engineering, manufacturing, clean-tech startups, innovation program management, consulting, and higher education. He's currently Clinical Faculty at Rice University's Jones Graduate School of Business, teaching design thinking and financial modeling. Before that he spent three years as Innovation Program Manager at Rice's Liu Idea Lab, where the work was heavily operational: program design, stakeholder management, building automation and database backends, and coaching 100+ startups per year.

He co-founded Crescendo Management, a consulting firm advising startups and small businesses on strategy, financial modeling, and fundraising. His career started in manufacturing and engineering in West Texas and the Bay Area before pivoting through an MBA at Rice into the intersection of entrepreneurship, technology, and program design.
