# Patrick Ray

## Who He Is

Patrick Ray is a Houston-based entrepreneurship educator, full-stack builder, and startup advisor. He's currently Clinical Faculty at Rice University's Jones Graduate School of Business, where he teaches MBA students design thinking and undergraduates financial modeling and venture planning. Before that he spent three years as Innovation Program Manager at Rice's Liu Idea Lab, coaching 100+ startups per year.

He's also a working country musician (Patrick B Ray), running a band with 85+ shows/year, three albums, and all business operations self-managed. He co-founded Crescendo Management, a consulting firm advising startups and small businesses on strategy, financial modeling, and fundraising.

His career started in mechanical engineering in West Texas and the Bay Area (venture-funded clean-tech startup), pivoted through an MBA at Rice, and landed in the intersection of entrepreneurship, education, and technology.
